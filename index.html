About

I like building software that people depend on, and I like being close to the people who use it. Right now that means building Heron Power's manufacturing execution system from scratch. It runs on Go, AWS, and Kubernetes and is used by 200+ people across finance, hardware, firmware, and manufacturing. I talk with technicians every day, and their feedback becomes what I ship next.

I study Technology and Information Management in the Honors School at UC Santa Cruz (Dean's Honor List, class of 2027). On campus, I build data pipelines for machine learning and generative media research, tutor CSE 12 students through assembly and memory bugs, and organize hackathons with NVIDIA, ASUS, Cisco, and AMD through Hack.dev. I compete in them too: SlugRoute won two prizes at CruzHacks, and Roost put an AI budgeting coach entirely on-device at the Qualcomm × LiteRT hackathon.
